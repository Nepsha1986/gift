International Women's Day is a chance to thank the women who make our lives better. But when your list includes your mom, partner, sister, best friend and a few colleagues, coming up with something special for each of them isn't easy. Here are five gifts that adapt to almost any personality and budget.

### 1. A Spa Night at Home

Put together a proper reset kit: a scented candle, bath salts or bath bombs, sheet masks, massage oil and a pair of cozy socks. The message is simple — "take a break, you've earned it." Arrange everything in a nice box and the unwrapping becomes part of the experience.

### 2. Jewelry With Meaning

It doesn't have to be expensive, just personal. An initial pendant, a delicate bracelet engraved with a meaningful date, or a ring set with her birthstone. Pieces like these get worn every day — and every time, she'll remember who they came from.

### 3. A Workshop Voucher

Give a new skill and a few hours of pure enjoyment instead of another object. Pottery, a paint-and-wine evening, Italian cooking, floristry, dance — match it to her interests. It's perfect for the person who "has everything," and if you go together, you'll share a memory too.

### 4. The Book She's Been Meaning to Read

A new release from her favorite author, a much-talked-about bestseller or a beautiful gift edition of a classic. Write a note on the inside cover — years from now, that inscription will mean more than anything else on the page. Not sure what to pick? A bookstore gift card always works.

### 5. A Subscription to Something She Loves

A music or streaming service, an online course, a monthly flower or coffee delivery. A subscription keeps the celebration going long after the day itself — with every new delivery or every new episode.

---

Whatever you choose, add a few warm words of your own. A handwritten card often moves people more than the gift itself. Happy International Women's Day! 🌷
