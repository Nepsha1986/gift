Giving clothes to a woman is a small act of courage. One wrong size and the gift turns into an awkward moment; one wrong style and it ends up at the back of the wardrobe with the tags still on. But a well-chosen piece — a soft scarf, a bag she's been eyeing, perfectly cozy loungewear — says "I notice you" better than almost anything else. **The secret is knowing where the risk is.**

## Risk Map

| Risk level | What to give | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Scarf, gloves, sunglasses, bag, hat, jewelry, watch | Size doesn't matter or is easy to guess |
| Medium | Sweater, cardigan, loungewear, pajamas, robe | Size matters, but a relaxed fit forgives mistakes |
| High | Dresses, jeans, lingerie, shoes, outerwear | Exact fit is a must — better to shop together or give a gift card |

## Accessories: The Safest Bet

- **Scarf or stole.** A silk scarf can be worn around the neck, in the hair or tied to a bag handle; a cashmere or merino stole keeps her warm all winter. Look at the colors she already wears and choose something that goes with her coat.
- **Bag.** A roomy **tote** for work and shopping, a compact **crossbody** for walks and travel, an elegant **clutch** for evenings out. Look at what she carries now: if she always has a laptop with her, a tiny clutch won't replace her main bag.
- **Sunglasses.** Make sure they have **UV400** protection — this matters more than the brand. Polarized lenses reduce glare on water and the road. If you're not sure about the frame shape, an optician's gift card is a stylish way out.
- **Hat.** A beret or a knitted beanie for autumn and winter, a wide-brim straw hat for a sea vacation, a bucket hat for city summers.
- **Watch.** A classic on a leather strap or a thin metal bracelet never goes out of style. Pay attention to the size of the case: on a slim wrist, 28–36 mm usually looks most elegant.
- **Leather gloves.** An underrated gift: they're needed every winter, and good ones last for years. Gloves are sized, but a quick look at her hand or her old pair is enough.

## Clothes: How Not to Miss the Size

- **Look at the labels** on the things she wears most often — not the newest ones. Take a photo of the label: you'll get both the size and the brand she likes.
- **Choose relaxed silhouettes.** An oversized sweater, a cardigan, a soft robe or a pajama set forgive small mistakes.
- **Prioritize the fabric.** Cashmere, merino, silk, quality cotton and linen feel noticeably different. A simple thing in great fabric beats a "trendy" thing in cheap synthetics.
- **Keep the receipt** and add a note: "If the size isn't right, we'll exchange it together." This removes all the awkwardness.

## Style: Respect Her Taste

- **Look at what she actually wears**, not what you'd like her to wear. If her wardrobe is jeans and sneakers, a sequined dress will most likely stay in the closet.
- **Pay attention to color.** Notice which colors keep showing up in her wardrobe — that's your safe palette.
- **Listen for hints.** Women often mention things they like: "I saw a cool bag," "my favorite sweater is worn out." Write it down — that's a ready-made gift idea.

## When It's Better to Shop Together

Dresses, jeans, lingerie and shoes are better tried on. Instead of guessing, give a **shopping day**: a gift card from her favorite store, your patient company and coffee or lunch afterwards. It's a gift, an outing and a date — all at once.

## Bottom Line

If you're not sure about the size, choose an **accessory**: a scarf, a bag, sunglasses, gloves or a watch. If you want to give clothing, pick a relaxed fit, good fabric and colors she already wears — and keep the receipt. For everything that needs a perfect fit, the best gift is shopping together.
