A home is a reflection of its owner. A woman who loves coziness has usually thought through every detail: the shade of the cushions, the scent in the hallway, the vase on the windowsill. That's why decor is such a delicate gift — a figurine that doesn't fit her style ends up in a drawer, while the right candle or a soft plaid becomes part of her everyday ritual. Here's how to hit the mark.

## First, Look Around

Before buying anything, pay attention to her home (or ask to see photos):

- **Style.** Scandinavian minimalism, boho with lots of textiles and plants, classic, loft? Every style has its own materials and colors.
- **Palette.** Which colors keep repeating — warm beige, cool gray, deep green, terracotta? A gift in the same palette fits in naturally.
- **Free space.** If every shelf is already full, choose something that is used up or used daily rather than something that needs a new spot.

## Safe Choices: Coziness Without Imposing Your Taste

- **Candles.** Soy or coconut wax burns cleaner and longer than paraffin, and a wooden wick crackles softly like a fireplace. Choose light, natural scents — linen, fig, sandalwood, citrus — sweet and heavy ones are a matter of taste.
- **Reed diffuser.** Scents the room for weeks without fire — perfect for the hallway or the bathroom.
- **Plaid or throw blanket.** Merino, cotton or a soft chunky knit: something you want to wrap yourself in on an autumn evening. Choose a neutral color — it will suit any sofa.
- **Cushion covers instead of cushions.** They're easy to change with the seasons, take up no extra space and let her refresh the interior in a minute.
- **A beautiful lamp or string lights.** Warm light (around 2700 K) instantly makes a room cozier. A small table lamp or a garland on the windowsill is a gift for every evening.

## Living Decor

- **A houseplant in a stylish pot.** If she doesn't have time for care, choose unpretentious plants: zamioculcas (ZZ plant), sansevieria (snake plant) or pothos — they forgive missed watering and low light.
- **A flower subscription.** A fresh bouquet every week or month is a gift that keeps on giving and never clutters the space.

## Personal Touches She Won't Find in a Store

- **Photo frame or photo book with your shared memories.** Print a photo in good quality and choose a frame to match her interior — a simple wooden or thin metal frame is a universal option.
- **A personalized poster.** A star map of a special date, a map of the city where you met, or a print of her favorite quote.
- **Handmade ceramics.** A vase, a mug set or a serving tray from a local ceramicist — each piece is unique and tells its own story.

## What's Risky

- **Paintings and wall art** — very personal; give them only if she has pointed out a specific piece.
- **Rugs, mirrors and large furniture** — they need exact dimensions and a good understanding of the space.
- **Figurines and decorative clocks** — easy to miss the style, and hard to get rid of without offending the giver.

If you have your eye on something risky, a **gift card to her favorite interior store** with a nice handwritten note is a perfectly elegant solution.

## Bottom Line

The best decor gift is one that adds coziness without demanding a new place on the shelf: candles, a diffuser, a plaid, cushion covers, warm light or a plant. Look at her home, stick to her palette — and add a personal touch, like a photo of your shared memories. Then your gift will become part of her home, not just another thing in it.
