Most men have a ritual: the phone gets propped on the sink, the volume goes to max, and the podcast still turns into mush the moment the water starts running. A shower speaker solves exactly that — and costs about as much as a nice bottle of whiskey. It's a small gift, but one he'll use every morning, which is more than you can say for most "big" presents.

## Who It's For

- **The podcast and audiobook guy** who doesn't want to pause the episode just because it's time to shower.
- **The morning-playlist man** who needs a beat to wake up.
- **The busy one** who takes calls anytime — many speakers have a microphone and an answer button.
- **The camper or fisherman.** A waterproof speaker works just as well at the campsite, in a boat or by the pool.

## What to Check Before You Buy

**1. Water protection: look for IPX7.**
This is the most important line in the specs. IPX4 only means "survives splashes". IPX5–IPX6 handles a direct stream of water. **IPX7** means the speaker survives being dropped into water up to 1 m (3 ft) deep — and in the shower it *will* get dropped. If the box just says "water-resistant" with no IP rating, keep looking.

**2. Mounting: suction cup, hook or strap.**
- A **suction cup** works only on smooth tile or glass. It won't stick to textured tile or a painted wall.
- A **hook or carabiner** is the most universal option: it hangs on the shower rod, a towel rail or a backpack.
- A **strap** is handy if the speaker will also travel.

Take a quick look at his bathroom before you buy and you'll know which one will actually hold.

**3. Sound: be realistic.**
A palm-sized speaker won't give you deep bass — and doesn't need to. What matters is that **speech is clear** (for podcasts and calls) and that it's loud enough over running water. Output of 5 W or more is usually plenty for a bathroom.

**4. Battery life.**
Look for at least 6–8 hours. That's a week of showers on a single charge. Check how it charges: **USB-C** is far more convenient than an old micro-USB port or a proprietary cable that will get lost.

**5. Buttons.**
Large, rubberized buttons for volume, skip and play/pause that you can press with wet hands without reaching for the phone. A nice bonus: a button that answers calls.

**6. Bluetooth 5.0 or newer.**
It holds the connection better through a door or wall when the phone stays in the bedroom.

## How to Make the Gift More Personal

- **Make a playlist** — "for the morning shower" or "songs from our first trip" — and share the link inside the card.
- **Bundle it:** speaker + a good shower gel or a set of soft towels. That's a ready "morning ritual" kit.
- **Add a podcast subscription** or a month of a streaming service so there's something to listen to.

## Bottom Line

A shower speaker is the rare case where an inexpensive gift brings joy every day. Pick a model with **IPX7 protection**, a mount that will actually hold in his bathroom, at least **6–8 hours** of battery and USB-C charging — and his mornings will start a little better.
